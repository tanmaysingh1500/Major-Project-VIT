# EV Registration & Road Tax (Illustrative Summary)

Unlike fuel/CNG vehicles, many Indian states have chosen to fully or
partially exempt electric vehicles from road tax and/or registration fees,
as a purchase-cost-reducing incentive layered on top of any direct subsidy.

Typical patterns seen across states (illustrative, not exhaustive):

- Full road tax exemption for private EVs for a defined incentive period.
- Reduced or waived registration fees compared to internal-combustion
  vehicles of a similar category.
- In some cases, exemptions or discounts also extended to green/white
  license plates issued specifically for EVs, which can carry additional
  benefits (e.g. parking or toll considerations in some cities).

Because these exemptions are state-specific and subject to periodic
revision, the EV Pal MVP's TCO calculator does not currently model road
tax/registration savings explicitly — this is left as a "Later" extension
once real, current, state-by-state rules are sourced and verified.
