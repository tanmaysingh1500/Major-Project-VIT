# FAME-II Scheme (Illustrative Summary)

FAME (Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Electric Vehicles) is a
central-government scheme intended to encourage the adoption of electric
and hybrid vehicles in India through demand incentives, along with support
for charging infrastructure.

The second phase, commonly referred to as FAME-II, broadly aimed to:

- Provide upfront purchase incentives on eligible electric vehicles,
  historically weighted toward electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and
  public/commercial-use four-wheelers rather than all private cars.
- Support the deployment of public charging infrastructure along highways
  and in cities.
- Encourage domestic manufacturing of EV components through
  localization requirements.

Over time, the scheme's incentive structure, eligible vehicle categories,
and subsidy amounts have been revised and phased in ways that changed
which vehicles qualify and by how much. Because of that, any specific
subsidy amount quoted in this MVP's sample data (see
`fame_ii_subsidy_structure.md`) should be treated as an illustrative
placeholder, not a current, actionable figure.

For an authoritative answer, consult the Ministry of Heavy Industries'
official FAME-II notifications and amendments directly.
