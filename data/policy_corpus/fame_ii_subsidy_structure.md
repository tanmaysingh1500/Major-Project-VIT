# Illustrative Subsidy Structure (Demo Placeholder)

For the purposes of this MVP demo only, EV Pal models a simplified central
subsidy as:

- A per-kWh incentive amount, applied to the vehicle's battery capacity.
- Capped at a maximum flat rupee amount per vehicle.
- Scoped to a narrower set of body types (in this demo, hatchbacks), loosely
  reflecting how FAME-II's real incentive structure historically favored
  smaller/mass-market vehicle categories and had a more limited scope for
  larger private four-wheelers.

This is a deliberate simplification. It is used so the deterministic TCO
calculator has a concrete, auditable rule to apply — not a claim about what
subsidy any specific real vehicle purchase would actually receive today.
Prospective buyers should check the current official scheme documents and
their dealer for real eligibility and amounts.
