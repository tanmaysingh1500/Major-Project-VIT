# Delhi EV Policy (Illustrative Summary)

Delhi has historically positioned itself as one of the more EV-forward
states in India, with a dedicated EV policy that has included, at various
points:

- Purchase incentives for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and
  four-wheelers, layered on top of any central FAME-II incentive.
- Waivers or reductions on road tax and registration fees for EVs.
- Targets for public charging station density (e.g. charging points within
  a short driving distance in dense urban areas).
- A scrappage incentive for retiring old polluting vehicles when buying an EV.

In this demo, Delhi is modeled as one of the states with a non-zero flat
state-level top-up subsidy in `data/tco_assumptions.json`. That number is a
simplified placeholder for demo purposes, not a sourced, current figure.
