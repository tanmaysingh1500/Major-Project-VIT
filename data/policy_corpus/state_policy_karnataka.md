# Karnataka EV Policy (Illustrative Summary)

Karnataka was among the first Indian states to introduce a dedicated EV and
energy storage policy, with a strong emphasis on attracting EV and battery
manufacturing investment into the state (it hosts a significant automotive
and electronics manufacturing base around Bengaluru).

Historically, Karnataka's approach has leaned more toward manufacturing
incentives, land and capital subsidies for EV/battery manufacturers, and
infrastructure support, with less emphasis on large direct purchase
subsidies for individual private buyers compared to some other states.

In this demo, Karnataka is modeled with a zero flat state purchase subsidy
in `data/tco_assumptions.json`, reflecting that difference in policy
emphasis — again, a simplification for demo purposes, not a verified,
current statement of Karnataka's policy.
