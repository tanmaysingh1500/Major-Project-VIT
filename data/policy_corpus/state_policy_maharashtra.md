# Maharashtra EV Policy (Illustrative Summary)

Maharashtra has run an EV policy aimed at encouraging both EV adoption and
local manufacturing, with elements that have historically included:

- Purchase incentives for private and commercial EVs, often with early-bird
  or first-movers' higher incentive tranches that reduce over time.
- Road tax and registration fee exemptions for electric vehicles.
- Support for setting up charging infrastructure, including in
  Mumbai/Pune-area urban clusters.

As with the other state summaries in this corpus, treat any numeric detail
here as illustrative background context rather than a current, actionable
subsidy figure.
